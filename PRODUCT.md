# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Members of a Final Fantasy XIV Free Company (guild). A mix of casual friends and organized raiders who coordinate events, track participation, manage lottery drawings, and plan fights/raids. They arrive from Discord, often on desktop, switching between the bot and the web app throughout their session. The tool supplements their gaming time, so it needs to stay out of the way and respect their attention.

## Product Purpose

A companion platform for FC management: event scheduling with signup flows, member directories synced from Discord and the Lodestone, lottery drawings, raid/fight resource management, and admin tools for officers. Success means members check the app habitually and officers run the FC without spreadsheets.

## Brand Personality

Sleek, capable, modern. The interface should feel like a well-crafted product tool, not a gaming skin. Confident without being cold, polished without being corporate. Think Linear's precision applied to a community coordination tool.

## Anti-references

- **Generic SaaS / corporate dashboards**: No bland admin templates, no cookie-cutter metric cards, no Stripe-clone aesthetics. This serves a community, not a business.
- **Over-the-top gaming UI**: No fantasy borders, no glowing neon accents, no heavy textures or themed chrome. The FFXIV connection is contextual (role colors, fight names), not decorative.

## Design Principles

1. **Tools, not toys**: Every element earns its place through utility. Decoration that doesn't aid comprehension gets cut.
2. **Respect the context switch**: Members come from Discord and the game. The UI should feel like a natural extension of that workflow, not a jarring departure.
3. **Clarity over density**: Breathe. Information-heavy screens (event lists, member tables, statistics) need deliberate hierarchy and spacing, not cramming.
4. **Community warmth through craft**: The personality comes from fit-and-finish (smooth transitions, considered typography, thoughtful empty states), not from theming or illustration.
5. **Fast by feel**: Perceived performance matters. Interactions should feel immediate; loading states should be purposeful, not absent.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA compliance. Focus-visible states on all interactive elements. Reduced motion support via `prefers-reduced-motion`. Sufficient contrast ratios (4.5:1 normal text, 3:1 large text). The existing setup covers the community's needs.
